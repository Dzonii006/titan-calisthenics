# Daily Posture & APT Reset Routine

*Perform once daily (morning upon waking or evening before bed). Takes ~6 minutes.*

## Routine
| # | Exercise | Sets & Reps / Duration | Execution Cue |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch** | 60s / side | Tuck tailbone under and squeeze rear glute tightly. Do not arch lower back. |
| **2** | **Glute Bridges with Pelvic Tuck** | 2 sets $\times$ 15 reps | Flatten lower back against the floor first, drive through heels, 2s glute pause at top. |
| **3** | **Deadbug (Flat Lumbar Spine)** | 2 sets $\times$ 10 reps / side | Lower back must remain glued flat to the floor at all times. Controlled breathing. |
| **4** | **Stomach Vacuum (TVA Bracing)** | 3 sets $\times$ 20s hold | Fully exhale all air, suck navel inward toward spine, hold 20s. |

---

## Standing Cue
- **Throughout the day**: Squeeze glutes ~20% and lightly draw navel in to keep pelvis neutral.
