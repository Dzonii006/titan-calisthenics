# Handstand Skill Progression Guide

*A 5-to-10 minute skill practice to perform when you are completely fresh (directly after warm-up, before main lifting).*

---

## The 3 Golden Rules
1. **Always Train Fresh**: Handstand is a balance and nervous system skill. Never practice it when shoulders, core, or wrists are fatigued.
2. **Chest-to-Wall, Not Back-to-Wall**: Facing the wall forces a straight hollow-body line and fixes pelvic tilt. Back-to-wall encourages an arched "banana back."
3. **The Finger Brakes ("Tiger Claw")**: Spread fingers wide and press fingertip pads firmly into the floor. When falling forward (toward your back), push hard through fingertips. When falling backward (toward your feet), press through the heels of your palms.

---

## 4-Phase Progression Roadmap

### Phase 1: Chest-to-Wall Handstand Hold
- **Setup**: Start in a push-up position with feet touching the wall. Walk feet up the wall while walking hands closer toward the wall.
- **Form**: Lock elbows straight, push shoulders up into ears (elevation), squeeze glutes, point toes.
- **Target**: Build up to **3 sets $\times$ 30–45s hold** with hands 15–25 cm from the wall.

---

### Phase 2: The Bail Out (Cartwheel / Pirouette Exit)
*Mastering this completely removes the fear of falling.*
- **Execution**: When you feel yourself falling past vertical, pick up one hand and step it forward/turn your torso sideways. Your legs will naturally cartwheel down to the side and land on your feet.
- **Target**: Practice deliberate bail-outs from a low wall walk 5–10 times until it feels automatic.

---

### Phase 3: Wall Pulls & Toe Taps (Micro-Balance)
- **Execution**: From a chest-to-wall hold (hands ~20 cm from wall):
  1. Gently squeeze your glutes and press your fingertips into the floor to peel your toes 2–3 cm off the wall.
  2. Hold the balance for 2–5 seconds before lightly tapping the wall again.
- **Target**: Accumulate **10 clean float-offs per session**.

---

### Phase 4: Freestanding Kick-Up & Balance
- **Setup**: Hands placed shoulder-width apart, fingers splayed. One leg forward, one leg back.
- **Execution**: Smoothly kick the back leg up while pushing through the shoulders. Catch the balance by actively working the fingers.
- **Target**: First **5–10 second freestanding hold** away from the wall.

---

## How to Program It
- **When**: 5–8 minutes at the start of **Workout A** and **Workout C** (right after the dynamic warm-up, before main sets).
- **Volume**: 3–5 quality attempts. Stop as soon as wrist/shoulder fatigue begins to drop your balance quality.
