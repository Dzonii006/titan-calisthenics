# Calisthenics & Rings Workout Program

## Schedule (4-Day Split)
- **Mon**: Workout A (Upper - Push/Pull & Delts)
- **Tue**: Workout B (Lower - Posterior Chain & Core)
- **Wed**: Rest / Active walk
- **Thu**: Workout C (Upper - Vertical Focus & Arms)
- **Fri**: Workout D (Lower - Quads, Glutes & Compression)
- **Sat/Sun**: Rest

---

## Workout A: Upper (Horizontal Push & Pull + Delts)

### Warm-Up (3–4 mins)
- Wrist rocks & wrist circles: 45s
- Big arm circles & crosses: 30s
- Scapular push-ups: 10 reps
- Ring support hold (feet on floor): 15s

### Workout
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gymnastic Ring Support Hold** | 3 | 15–20s | 60s | Elbows fully locked, rings turned slightly outward. |
| **Ring Inverted Rows** | 3 | 8–12 | 90s | Body in straight plank, pull chest to rings, 1s pause at top. |
| **Ring Push-ups** | 3 | 8–12 | 90s | Turn rings outward at top of lockout. |
| **Parallel Dip Bar Dips** | 3 | 6–10 | 90s | Bar rests on heel of palms; lean torso slightly forward. |
| **DB Lateral Raises** | 4 | 12–15 | 60s | Strict, lead with elbows, 1s pause at shoulder height. |
| **DB Hammer Curls** | 3 | 10–12 | 60s | Neutral grip (palms facing each other), controlled lower. |

## Workout B: Lower (Posterior Chain & Core)

### Warm-Up (3–4 mins)
- Bodyweight squats: 12 reps
- Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch: 45s / side
- Glute bridges: 12 reps with 2s squeeze at top

### Workout
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DB Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)** | 4 | 10–12 | 90s | Hips drive back, soft knees, hard glute squeeze at top. |
| **DB Bulgarian Split Squats** | 3 | 8–10 / leg | 90s | Rear foot elevated on chair/couch, lower until front thigh is parallel. |
| **DB Standing Calf Raises** | 3 | 15–20 | 45s | Full stretch at bottom, 2s peak hold on tiptoes. |
| **Hanging Knee/Leg Raises** | 3 | 10–12 | 60s | Strict—no swinging. Curl pelvis upward toward ribs. |
| **Hollow Body Hold** | 3 | 25–35s | 45s | Lower back pressed flat into floor; engage core tight. |

---

## Workout C: Upper (Vertical Push/Pull & Delts)

### Warm-Up (3–4 mins)
- Wrist rocks: 30s
- Deadhang + Scapular shrugs on bar: 15s hang + 8 shrugs
- Push-up to downward dog: 6 reps

### Workout
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strict Pull-ups / Chin-ups** | 4 | 4–8 | 2 mins | Full deadhang at bottom, drive elbows down to ribs. |
| **Ring Dips** (or Bar Dips) | 3 | 6–8 | 90s | Keep rings tight to ribs on descent; lock out at top. |
| **Pike Push-ups** | 3 | 8–10 | 90s | Hips high in inverted V; head lowers forward like a triangle. |
| **Ring Archer or Wide Push-ups** | 3 | 8–10 | 90s | Controlled descent; flare lats. |
| **DB Rear Delt Flyes** | 3 | 12–15 | 60s | Bent over, pull with back of shoulders, no torso swinging. |
| **Ring Triceps Extensions** | 3 | 10–12 | 60s | Body angled, keep elbows pinned high, press through palms. |

---

## Workout D: Lower (Quads, Glutes & Compression)

### Warm-Up (3–4 mins)
- Leg swings (front/back & side/side): 10 / leg
- Deep squat hold: 30s
- Glute bridge hold: 20s

### Workout
| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Key Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DB Goblet Squats** | 4 | 10–12 | 90s | DB vertical at chest; deep squat between knees, chest tall. |
| **Single-Leg DB Glute Bridges** | 3 | 12 / leg | 60s | DB on hips; drive through heel, 2s squeeze at lockout. |
| **DB Walking Lunges** | 3 | 12 total | 60s | Upright posture, controlled steps. |
| **Dip Bar Tuck L-Sit Hold** | 4 | 10–15s | 60s | Depress shoulders down, knees tucked at 90 degrees. |
| **Plank with Posterior Pelvic Tilt** | 3 | 45s | 45s | Squeeze glutes hard and tuck tailbone under. |

## Quick Scaling Guide
- **Push-ups**: Incline (easier) $\leftarrow$ Floor $\rightarrow$ Rings Turned Out (harder).
- **Inverted Rows**: High angle $\leftarrow$ Feet flat $\rightarrow$ Feet elevated.
- **Dips**: Bench/Feet assisted $\leftarrow$ Dip Bar $\rightarrow$ Ring Dips.
- **Pull-ups**: Negative lowers (5s) $\leftarrow$ Chin-ups $\rightarrow$ Strict Pull-ups.
- **Progression Rule**: When you can hit the top rep count across all sets, increase reps, angle difficulty, or DB weight.
