# Nutrition Reference Sheet

## Hand-Portion System
| Portion | Macro Category | Est. Amount | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 Palm** | Lean Protein | ~25–30g | Chicken, lean beef, eggs, cottage cheese, fish |
| **1 Fist** | Vegetables | Micronutrients/Fiber | Broccoli, spinach, salad, cucumbers, zucchini |
| **1 Cupped Hand** | Carbs | ~25–30g | Rice, potatoes, oats, whole-grain bread, fruit |
| **1 Thumb** | Fats | ~7–10g | Olive oil, nuts, peanut butter, cheese, avocado |

---

## Daily Rules
1. **Protein Target**: 130–145g / day (1–1.5 palms per meal + 1 scoop whey daily).
2. **Half-Plate Veggies**: Lunch and dinner.
3. **1 Cupped Hand Carbs**: Per main meal (adjust based on progress).
4. **Zero-Liquid Calories**: Water, black coffee, green tea, zero-calorie drinks only. Target: 2.5–3L water/day.

---

## Daily Meal Template
- **Breakfast**: 1 Palm protein + 1 Fist veg + 1 Cupped hand carbs
- **Lunch**: 1.5 Palms protein + 2 Fists veg + 1 Cupped hand carbs + 1 Thumb fat
- **Snack / Post-Workout**: 1 Scoop Whey Protein + 1 fruit
- **Dinner**: 1.5 Palms protein + 2 Fists veg + 1 Cupped hand carbs + 1 Thumb fat

---

## Bi-Weekly Adjustment Guide
- **Waist shrinking & weight stable**: Keep portions exactly as they are.
- **Waist unchanged & weight increasing**: Remove 1/2 cupped hand of carbs from dinner.
- **Weight dropping too fast (>0.5kg/week) & fatigue**: Add 1 cupped hand of carbs or 1 thumb of fat.
